## Hosting, delivery, security and web analytics by Cloudflare

This website is delivered and protected through Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA ("Cloudflare"). When a page is requested, Cloudflare processes connection and request data as necessary to deliver, secure and reliably operate the website. This may include the IP address, date and time of the request, the requested URL, the referring website, and technical information about the browser and device. Cloudflare may also process technical security and network diagnostic data.

The source code of this website does not contain its own analytics or advertising service. The controller may use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregated visit and performance statistics for this website. Whether and for which visitors Web Analytics actually runs depends on the domain settings in Cloudflare; Cloudflare can automatically inject the beacon into pages at the server. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics is enabled by default on free domains; the default setting excludes data from visitors in the EU. To collect visits from the EU as well, Web Analytics must be enabled globally in Cloudflare. If Web Analytics is enabled, it collects page-load performance data and aggregated visit data. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage and does not track individual users across different websites or over time. For more information, see the [Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/data-origin-and-collection/) and [Cloudflare's announcement about enabling it by default](https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-rum-diaries-enabling-web-analytics-by-default/).

If Web Analytics is used, its purpose is to measure aggregate use and performance of this website. To the extent that personal data are processed and consent is not required, the legal basis is the controller's legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, namely understanding and improving the website's reach and technical performance. You may object to this processing on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). Where consent is required, processing takes place only with your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where applicable, § 25(1) TDDDG. Cloudflare's statements about cookies and tracking do not automatically rule out a consent requirement under § 25 TDDDG for accessing information on a user's device. Collection of visits from the EU is only enabled once the applicable requirements, including any required consent, are met.

The legal basis for processing necessary to deliver and secure the website is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The controller's legitimate interest is the secure, reliable and efficient operation of this website.

Cloudflare may also process data in countries outside the European Economic Area. Retention periods depend on the Cloudflare service and account settings. Information about the data protection commitments and transfer mechanisms Cloudflare describes for its customers is available in the [Cloudflare Customer Data Processing Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) and the [list of subprocessors](https://www.cloudflare.com/gdpr/subprocessors/). The contractual terms and transfer mechanisms that apply to this website depend on the terms agreed for the Cloudflare account.
